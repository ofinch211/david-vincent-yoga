A PERSONAL JOURNEY

It started with
showing up.

David began practising yoga in April 2022. What started as an interest became a lasting passion for movement, breath and the philosophy behind the practice. Alongside his career in investment banking, he is developing an independent teaching practice centred on personal attention and consistent, thoughtful learning.

By June 2025, David had accumulated more than 200 hours of teaching experience. Alongside his own practice, sharing yoga with others has become an important part of his journey.

David holds a recognised yoga teaching certification from The Yoga People. He trained with The Yoga People, a Yoga Alliance Registered Yoga School.

His interests centre on Ashtanga Vinyasa and Dharma Yoga: two distinct approaches that offer rich ways to explore movement, breathing and awareness.

David continues to learn, practise and develop. His intention is to create a welcoming environment for private lessons and small groups, with space for questions and guidance that responds to each person.

DAVID’S APPROACH

Thoughtful guidance.
Room to be you.

Consistency over perfection. Curiosity over comparison.

Every person arrives with a different body, experience and intention. Sessions begin with listening, then build from a suitable starting point. Progress is something to explore over time, rather than a pose to achieve.

David’s sessions will take place in and around Sanderstead, South Croydon. The exact venue will be confirmed before your session. Enquire about what could work for you.
