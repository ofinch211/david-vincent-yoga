About this preview

David Vincent is responsible for David Vincent Yoga enquiry correspondence. Contact dlvincent@hotmail.co.uk or 07956 167 856 with questions about your information.

This is a public design preview hosted on GitHub Pages. It does not include an enquiry submission form, online booking, payment collection or a student database. Business correspondence address and final booking arrangements remain to be added before a full business launch.

Contacting David

Email and telephone links let you contact David directly. An email link opens your own email application and does not send a message automatically. Any contact details and information you choose to send are used to respond and discuss a possible session. Please do not send sensitive medical information in an initial enquiry.

Session enquiries are handled to take steps at your request before a possible contract. General correspondence is handled on the basis of legitimate interests in responding to questions, balanced against your privacy rights. David’s email provider processes messages for delivery and storage. No enquiry information is sold or used to add you to marketing lists.

Hosting and browser preferences

GitHub provides the hosting for this preview and may process technical connection information, including IP addresses, to deliver and secure the service. Read GitHub’s privacy statement for its processing and international transfer information.

This website adds no analytics, advertising cookies or social media embeds. Fonts and images are included with the site. Choosing a colour palette or day/dark mode saves that choice in this browser’s local storage. The preference is not sent to David and is not used for tracking. “Restore original” returns the original day palette; clear this site’s browser data to remove the saved preference.

Retention and your rights

The default policy for routine enquiry correspondence is to retain it for up to six months after the last contact, unless needed for an ongoing request, a dispute or a legal obligation. This is reviewed manually. GitHub’s technical data retention is governed by its own privacy statement.

Depending on the circumstances, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, data portability or object to processing based on legitimate interests. Rights are subject to legal conditions and exemptions. Contact David using the details above. You may also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office.

Changes

This notice will be updated before any enquiry forms, booking tools or additional providers are introduced. External websites have their own privacy practices.
