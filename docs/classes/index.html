BEFORE YOUR FIRST SESSION

A few things
you may wonder.

Do I need experience?

Beginners are welcome, subject to suitability for the session. Tell David a little about your experience so you can discuss an appropriate starting point together.

Where will sessions take place?

In and around Sanderstead, South Croydon. The exact hall or studio will be confirmed before your session. David is developing flexible teaching arrangements rather than operating from a permanent studio.

How much does a session cost?

Private sessions start from £40. Small-group sessions are approximately £30 per person. Final prices, duration and availability are confirmed when you enquire. This website does not collect payments.

Is my enquiry a confirmed booking?

No. An enquiry starts a conversation. Your session is only agreed once you and David have confirmed the details directly.

What should I bring?

Wear comfortable clothes you can move in. Mat and equipment arrangements will be discussed before your session.
