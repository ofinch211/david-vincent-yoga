THE APPROACH OF SRI DHARMA MITTRA

A holistic
exploration.

Dharma Yoga was developed by Sri Dharma Mittra, drawing on classical Hatha and Raja Yoga. It brings physical postures together with breath practices, concentration, meditation and ethical principles.

Its purpose extends beyond a physical sequence. Compassion, devotion and self-awareness inform a practice concerned with the relationship between body, mind and spirit.

A PRACTICE THAT DEVELOPS

Why practise
Dharma Yoga?

A way to explore physical practice and the wider philosophy of yoga together.

  • Develop strength, flexibility, balance and coordination
  • Explore breathing and meditation
  • Cultivate concentration and self-awareness
  • Deepen your understanding of yoga philosophy

Everyone’s experience is different. Sessions are adapted through conversation and suitable guidance; no particular physical or therapeutic outcome is promised.
