THE SEQUENCES

Progressive.
Never a race.

The Primary Series, traditionally called Yoga Chikitsa, introduces a sequence of standing and seated postures, followed by finishing postures. Its traditional name is not a promise of medical treatment.

Intermediate and Advanced series explore further ranges of movement and demanding transitions. Progression depends on individual readiness and guidance, rather than a deadline or a need to complete every posture.

WAYS TO LEARN

Mysore-style practice

Students work through an appropriate part of the sequence at their own breathing pace, with individual guidance in a shared room. Beginners can learn gradually rather than memorising everything at once.

Led classes

A teacher guides the group through the breath and movement count. David’s available session format and the appropriate starting point can be discussed when you enquire.
