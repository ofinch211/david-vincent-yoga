PRIVACY & COOKIES

A thoughtful approach
to your information.

Draft for the local enquiry website · Updated 11 November 2026

Who is responsible

David Vincent is responsible for personal information used for David Vincent Yoga enquiries. For privacy questions or to exercise your rights, email {{ site.email }} or call {{ site.phone }}.

This is currently a local development website. The business correspondence address, public hosting provider and notification arrangements must be added before launch. This notice describes the current setup; it will be updated when those arrangements are confirmed.

Information we collect

If you enquire, we collect your name, email address, optional telephone number, preferred practice, optional date and time, experience level and any message you choose to provide. We also record when the enquiry was received and your acknowledgement of this notice. Required fields are needed to handle your request; the other fields are optional.

The server may record basic request information such as IP address, request time, page requested and response status for operating and troubleshooting the website. The application does not intentionally write form contents to those logs. Please do not include medical or other sensitive personal information in the enquiry form.

Why we use it

Session enquiries are used to respond, discuss suitability and take steps at your request before a possible contract. General correspondence and basic security or troubleshooting records are handled on the basis of legitimate interests in responding to questions and operating a reliable website, balanced against your privacy rights. No automated decisions or profiling are used.

We do not sell your information or add you to marketing lists because you made an enquiry. Any future marketing signup would be separate and optional. If you choose not to supply the required enquiry details, you can still browse the site or contact David directly.

Storage and who can see it

In this preview, form submissions are saved in a local database on the computer running the site, accessible to the website operator for handling enquiries. They are not published. Email notifications are not yet connected, so saving the form does not automatically email David or confirm an appointment.

If you contact David directly by email, the email providers involved process the message for delivery and storage. Information may also be disclosed where legally required. When public hosting or notification services are selected, their roles and any international transfer arrangements will be described here before they receive form data.

How long information is kept

The default retention policy for routine enquiries is up to six months after the last contact, unless a longer period is reasonably needed for an ongoing request, a dispute or a legal obligation. Retention is reviewed manually; the current application does not automatically delete old records. Booking, payment or other records will have appropriate retention periods explained if those services are introduced.

Technical logs should be kept only as long as needed for troubleshooting and security. The specific log retention period and backup arrangements will be finalised with the hosting setup before launch.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances and lawful basis, you may ask for access to your information, correction, deletion or restricted processing. You may also have a right to data portability and to object to processing based on legitimate interests. These rights are subject to legal conditions and exemptions. If future processing relies on consent, you can withdraw it without affecting earlier lawful processing.

Contact David using the details above. We may need to check your identity before sharing personal information. You can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office, the UK data protection regulator, through its website.

Cookies and local assets

The site uses an essential session cookie to protect the form and display submission results. It is a session cookie rather than a marketing or analytics identifier. There are no advertising cookies, analytics tools or embedded social feeds. Fonts and images are served locally. A separate choice would be offered before introducing non-essential cookies.

When you choose a day/dark mode or colour palette using the appearance controls, that preference is saved in this browser’s local storage so it can be remembered on your next visit. It is not sent to David and is not used for tracking. “Restore original” returns the original day palette; you can remove the saved preference by clearing this site’s browser data.

Links and changes

External links open independent websites with their own privacy practices. This notice will be updated if the way information is handled changes. The date above shows the latest revision.
